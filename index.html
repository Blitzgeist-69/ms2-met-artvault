About Met Art Vault

Met Art Vault is an interactive web application that lets you explore the vast public collection of The Metropolitan Museum of Art.

Purpose

The site was created to make it easy and enjoyable for anyone to discover, learn about, and save their favourite artworks from The Met's collection of over 470,000 pieces.

How to Use the Site
  • Search - Type any keyword (artist, title, or subject) and press Enter or click the search icon.
  • Surprise Me! - Click this button to discover random artworks from the collection.
  • View Details - Click any artwork card to open a modal with more information (artist, date, medium, department).
  • Save to Art Vault - Click “Save to Art Vault” in the modal to add artworks to your personal collection.
  • View Your Vault - Click “Art Vault” in the navigation bar to see all the artworks you've saved.
  • Dark Mode - Toggle between light and dark theme using the button in the navbar.

Note: All saved artworks are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. They will not be saved if you clear your browser data or use a different device.
